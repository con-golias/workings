# Method: humanoid robots at work in 2026

**Rule.** A number is used only from the company's own filing or statement, an analyst's own release, or a government
notice (primary), or from a named press report of one (secondary, labelled in `data/inputs.json` and attributed in the
text). Read on 21–22 November 2026.

## Shipments and uses (IDC)

idc.com/resource-center/blog). H1 2026 global shipments "接近2.5万台", +432.1%, market over USD 740 million. China's
market: over 19,000, 77.9% of the global market (a destination share). Chinese vendors: over 95% of global shipments.

say whether the share is of the world or of China's market; with China at 77.9% of the world, even the China-only
reading puts more than half of world shipments in those uses. 2030 forecast: over 750,000. H1 2025 (≈4,700) is
derived from the growth rate. 2025 full year: Omdia 13,318 (Jan 2026, via SCMP/TechNode; it ranks AgiBot first),

## Definitions
- **Capacity**: each maker's own stated capacity (planned or built), as in fig5; Tesla's is an "eventual planned" figure.
  Five makers only. Unitree's "75,000 humanoids a year within five years" (RoboStrategy, citing the 31 Jul 2026 issuance
  announcement) is not in the issuance announcement's text, the final prospectus (its ¥624M manufacturing-base project
  gives no unit figure) or the 729-page prospectus annex; the annex's legal opinion lists an approved "年产3万台机器人"
  project (30,000 robots a year, all kinds). Not added.
- **Shipped a year**: IDC's H1 2026 × 2.
- **Working year**: 2,000 hours (40 × 50). A robot hour is not a person's hour of output; this flatters the robots.
- **Figure at BMW**: Figure does not say how many robots shared the 1,250 hours; they match one 10-hour shift a day for
  about six months.
- **Money**: one illustrative rate, about 7.1 yuan per dollar.

## Limits
Shipped, sold, installed and ordered are different counts, and the analysts differ by about 40% on 2025. The three
Chinese filings cover roughly 40–50% of 2025's units. Company words ("deployed", "committed", "on-the-job training") are
the companies'. No maker publishes how many robots work each shift. A snapshot: 21 November 2026.
