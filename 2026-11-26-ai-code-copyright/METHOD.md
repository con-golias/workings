# Method

## Source
US Copyright Office Public Records System, through the API its website uses (`api.publicrecords.copyright.gov/
search_service_external/`): `simple_search_dsl` finds records (keyword search, filters for type of work and
registration date); `search_unique_record` returns the full record, including `material_excluded_statement`,
`material_included_list`, `author_statement_list` and `copyright_note`. Read 25 November 2026.

## How records were found
1. **Phrases** (code/fetch.py): the 11 AI phrases of the 20 November count, plus 37 for code (Copilot, "Cursor", Codex,
   "Claude Code", "AI agent", "AI assistant", "code generated", "large language model", "auto-generated" and others).
2. **Sweep** (code/sweep.py): 20 broad words (AI, generated, generative, agent, assisted, automated, model, tool,
   artificial, LLM, copilot, machine, Claude, Codex, Cursor, Gemini, GPT, Anthropic, OpenAI, Windsurf) restricted to
   Computer Files registered from October 2023.
3. **Random sample** (code/baseline.py): 600 Computer Files registrations, January 2025 to March 2026, 4 random pages
   of 10 per month from a fixed seed.
4. **Full read** (code/fullread.py): every Computer Files registration from October 2023 to November 2026, each full
   record read (count in summary.json `full_read`). It found no AI-code registration the other three had missed.
- **"C.O. correspondence"** is a note in the record; the catalogue does not define it. It is read here as a sign that
  the Office and the applicant corresponded during examination.

## What counts
- The **Material Excluded** statement names AI (the phrase list in `code/analysis.py`, including Swift Assist) and an
  AI-bearing piece of it names code; or "AI-generated <…code…>"; or "<…code…> generated by artificial intelligence"
  within one clause. AI in a title or a claimant's name does not count.
- "Machine-generated" alone does not count for code (code generators and compilers).
- Hand decisions, each in the code with its reason: one added (TX0009596268), two dropped (TX0009635300: the code was
  preexisting; TX0009525239: "OpenAI Base Code" is OpenAI's code, not AI output).

## Checks
- Every statement the rule came close to (AI or tool words plus code words, not counted) was read: 13 added (Swift
  Assist ×6, "<code> and <x> generated by artificial intelligence", one misspelling, Copilot "test cases").
- Recall on the random sample: 18 AI-code registrations; the phrases had found 17, the sweep the 18th.
- Bridge to the 20 November count (186 after its correction): 185 kept, 1 dropped, 82 added.
- Rate: Computer Files only; a quarter counts as complete when its Computer Files count reaches 80% of 2025's median
  quarter (records reach the catalogue late).
- Organisation = a company-like claimant name, or an author marked "employer for hire". People are counted, never named;
  their claimant names and titles are not in the public data.
- "C.O. correspondence" compared like with like: same months, same type of work, organisations and people separately.

## Limits
- Disclosure, not use. Few companies register software at all.
- Text-type registrations rest on the phrase search alone.
- "C.O. correspondence" does not say what the correspondence was about.
- 2026 is incomplete; batches (Adobe, IBM, Applied Systems, Apple) move quarters.
- Not legal advice.
